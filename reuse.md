*Last updated: July 25, 2026*

**Short version: take it.** Everything written on this site is licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). You can copy it, reprint
it, translate it, rewrite it, and use it commercially. You don't need to ask us, and there
is no fee.

Two conditions: **credit us**, and **keep your version as open as this one**.

We wrote this to be useful to people, and information that stays on one website helps
fewer people than information that spreads.

## Just need the phone numbers? Take them.

**Facts aren't copyrightable, and no license reaches them.** Phone numbers, hours, income
limits, filing deadlines, eligibility figures — take any of it, put it in anything, under
any terms you like. Nothing below applies to you.

If you run a clinic, a school, an agency, or a helpline and you want our hotline list in
your own intake packet, you are already free to do that. The license only matters if you
are copying our **writing**.

## What you can do with the writing

- Print a hotline list for a waiting room, a shelter, or a support group
- Put a benefits guide in an intake packet or a classroom handout
- Translate a page into a language we don't cover
- Quote it in a newsletter, a zine, a training deck, or a book
- Build it into an app or a service, including one you charge for
- Correct it, shorten it, or rewrite it for your own audience

## What we ask in return

**1. Credit.** Something like this is plenty:

> Adapted from Disability Wiki (https://disabilitywiki.org), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Say if you changed something, and don't suggest we endorse you.

**2. Keep it open (ShareAlike).** If you adapt, remix, or build on this writing, your
version has to carry the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. You can sell it; you just can't close
it. Nobody should be able to take a community resource, lock it up, and charge people for
access to what was given freely.

This applies to **adaptations**. Quoting a page in something larger, or handing out an
unmodified printout, doesn't put the rest of your work under this license.

## Please date your copy

This is a request, not a rule, and it matters more than the credit does.

Benefit rules change. Legal deadlines change. Crisis phone numbers get disconnected,
and services quietly cut their hours. A printed copy that looks current but is two
years stale can send someone to a dead number at the worst possible moment — that is
worse than handing them nothing.

So: put a date on your copy, and point people back to the live page. **If you are
reprinting crisis information, check it against this site before each new print run.**
Our own crisis pages carry the date they were last reviewed for this reason.

## The parts that aren't ours to give

- **Quoted material** stays with whoever owns it. Where a page quotes a law, a
  standard, or an organization's own wording, that piece isn't ours to relicense.
- **Logos and names** — ours and other organizations' — aren't covered. The license
  covers writing, not trademarks.

## One thing this isn't

A license is permission to copy. It is not a warranty, and it isn't advice. Nothing on
this site is legal, medical, or financial advice, and reusing a page doesn't transfer
any guarantee — from us to you, or from you to your readers. Check anything that
someone's housing, benefits, or safety depends on.
